## Holidays

**Coverage:** 2025-01-01..2026-12-31 (inclusive trade dates). Tier: T1 throughout.

One table serves the `b3` venue: B3 publishes one equities calendar per year and the crate routes the cash-equity venue to it. Two artifacts key the rows: the operator's 2025 calendar article `Calendário de feriados 2025` (published 2024-12-04) together with the Ofício Circular 149/2024-PRE it links, and the operator's 2026 calendar article `Calendário de negociação da B3: confira o funcionamento da bolsa em 2026` (published 2026-01-09). The document ids record which: `B3-OC-149-2024-PRE` for the circular, `B3-NEWS-CAL-2026` for the 2026 article. Both articles state, in session language, that no equities session runs on the listed dates — 2025: `não haverá negociação para os mercados de renda variável`; 2026: `estarão suspensas as operações com ações …`, with 24 December and 31 December stated separately (`Não haverá sessão de negociação`, `não haverá sessão de negociação na B3`).

Holidays that fell on weekends in 2025 — 07 September, 12 October, 02 November, 15 November — print no session change and ship no row. The operator's own statement that São Paulo holidays no longer close the market (`Desde o calendário de 2022, a bolsa do Brasil não para durante os feriados municipais e estaduais de São Paulo`) leaves 2025-07-09 and 2026-07-09 audited normal.

**Gaps, 2025-2026:** none inside the window. Every equities trade date the operator's two artifacts modify ships a row, and every other date inside the window is audited normal.

**Gaps, 2027:** the operator has published no 2027 calendar. Verified 2026-09-28 UTC: B3 released the 2025 calendar on 2024-12-04 and the 2026 calendar on 2026-01-09, and no 2027 article, circular or market calendar existed at retrieval. Nothing is being withheld by this crate — there is no 2027 calendar yet. **Closing condition:** the operator's 2027 calendar publication, at which point the window extends to 2027-12-31. Re-checked monthly per LAW-WATCH: the identity is holiday-bearing, so its cadence is monthly.

**Interpretive steps.** The two Ash Wednesday rows are **late opens**, not early closes and not closures: the operator states a special-horário day whose equities continuous session runs 13:00-17:55 (2025, Anexo II) or whose `negociações começando às 13h` (2026), and both dates fall while the United States is on standard time, so the long grid is in force and the normal 10:00 open moves to 13:00 while the normal 17:55 close is unchanged. `LateOpen { 13:00 }` states exactly that. What the scalar cannot state is the 12:45-13:00 pre-opening (order collection) window both artifacts print beside it: it is not an executable phase, it starts before the late open and is therefore clipped, and the day's executable instants are all stated exactly. The withheld window is recorded here as a residual, not a coverage gap — the operator's executable arrangement ships complete. This reading is fenced per date in `tests/global_equities/holidays.rs`.

## Gaps and residual risks

- **executable, pre-opening window on the 11:00-open grids.** The 2010–2012 long grids carry no pre-opening phase. The 2010–2012 circulars pin the trading grid itself and the 2025 circular proves the pre-opening concept in force at the 2026-08-24 review, but nothing in the audited primary set states the pre-opening window for those grids; a 10:45–11:00 phase would be an inference from the fifteen-minute convention of the 2026-08-24 review, so under LAW-PRIMARY-SOURCES the window is omitted and reads closed. Closing condition: a B3 circular or annex for the 2010–2012 era that states the pre-opening window in session language.
- **Recurring regime.** From 2015-12-21 the profile is not a revision row but a recurring selector: circular 127/2015-DP ties the short and long grids to the Brazil/New York daylight-time relationship, so `profile_at` picks the short grid when São Paulo sits one hour behind its usual offset from New York. The 2015-12-21 constant lives in `b3.rs` as `REFERENCE_GRID` and is not a `revisions!` tuple.
- **Interpretive step, order-entry classification.** The fifteen minutes before the open collect and price a book that does not match and the first print is the opening call, so that leg is `order_entry`. The immediately preceding "Order Cancellation" window, 09:30–09:45, stays excluded from the model entirely. The remaining phases all print: closing calls cross at the auction price and the after-market windows execute.
- **Interpretive step, after-market envelopes.** The after-market envelopes merge the venue's after-market pre-opening with after-market trading rather than splitting them on an unsourced internal boundary, so they stay tradeable whole.
- **Service tier.** The consumer serves this venue live (it is one of the market-clock overview's venues), so the identity is **served** and the holiday-bearing calendar is reviewed monthly per LAW-WATCH; the 2027 closing condition above is the one tracked gap.
