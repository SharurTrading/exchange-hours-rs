## Holidays

**Coverage:** 2011-01-01..2024-12-31 and 2025-01-01..2026-12-31 (inclusive trade dates; two audited windows). Tier: T1 throughout.

One table serves the `b3` venue: B3 publishes one equities calendar per year and the crate routes the cash-equity venue to it. The 2025-2026 rows key to the operator's 2025 calendar article `Calendário de feriados 2025` (published 2024-12-04) together with the Ofício Circular 149/2024-PRE it links, and the operator's 2026 calendar article `Calendário de negociação da B3: confira o funcionamento da bolsa em 2026` (published 2026-01-09); the document ids are `B3-OC-149-2024-PRE` and `B3-NEWS-CAL-2026`. Both articles state, in session language, that no equities session runs on the listed dates — 2025: `não haverá negociação para os mercados de renda variável`; 2026: `estarão suspensas as operações com ações …`, with 24 December and 31 December stated separately (`Não haverá sessão de negociação`, `não haverá sessão de negociação na B3`).

The 2011-2024 rows were backfilled on 2026-09-29 (UTC) from the operator's own yearly announcements and calendar pages, read as Internet Archive `id_` replays of the operator's pages (T1 via the verbatim-mirror rule). Four artifact generations key them:

- **2011-2012, 2015:** the operator's own news articles — `Confira o calendário de feriados do mercado para 2011` (published 2010-11-05), `BM&FBOVESPA divulga o calendário 2012` (2011-11-29) and `BM&FBOVESPA divulga calendário de feriados de 2015` (2014-11-19) — each stating `Não haverá negociação nos mercados da Bolsa nas seguintes datas` / `Não haverá negociação nos segmentos de renda variável, de renda fixa privada e de derivativos … nas seguintes datas:` and listing the dates in session language. The 2012 announcement is corroborated by the operator's own `Calendário do Mercado 2012` page (`B3-REG-CAL-2012`), whose detail tables repeat the same dates.
- **2013-2014:** the operator's `Calendário do Mercado` page under Regulação (`B3-REG-CAL-2013`, `B3-REG-CAL-2014-FEB`, `B3-REG-CAL-2014-MAR`), whose header `Dias em que não haverá negociação na Bolsa em <year>` is followed by per-day rows; the 2013 page states per day `Não haverá negociação nos segmentos de renda variável, de renda fixa privada e de derivativos.`, and the 2014 page's detail rows repeat that sentence while its summary block prints the day icons (`ic_03.gif`, `ic_04.gif`) beside the two Carnaval labels.
- **2016-2018:** the operator's PUMA trading-calendar `Feriados` page (`B3-PUMA-FER-2016`, `B3-PUMA-FER-2017`, `B3-PUMA-FER-2018`, with the 2018-12-24 capture `B3-PUMA-FER-2018-DEC` repeating the December table), under the same `Dias em que não haverá negociação na Bolsa` header; the 2016 article `Bolsa divulga datas e dinâmica de funcionamento do mercado em 2016` (2015-12-09, `B3-NEWS-CAL-2016`) corroborates.
- **2019-2024:** the same PUMA page, which from 2019 states the equities leg explicitly per day — `Não haverá negociação nos mercados de renda variável, renda fixa privada e de derivativos listados …` (2019-2020) and `… ETF de renda fixa …` (2021-2024). The 2024-02-27 capture embeds the 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024 calendars in one artifact (`B3-PUMA-FER-2021-2024`).

Each year's Ash Wednesday is keyed to the operator's own special-hours statement — the announcements and pages print `a negociação … terá início às 13h` (2011-2013, 2016-2024) and the hours notices `B3-NEWS-CINZAS-2014` (published 2014-02-27) and `B3-NEWS-CINZAS-2015` print `Das 12h45 às 13h – fase de pré-abertura. Das 13h às 17h – fase de negociação` for 2014-03-05 and `Das 13h às 16h55 – sessão contínua de negociação` for 2015-02-18.

**2010 is an unaudited span.** No operator artifact stating the 2010 holiday arrangement was found at the 2026-09-29 retrieval (search record in the research store's `holidays/raw/equities/b3/2010-2024/INDEX.md`), so the backfill window starts at 2011-01-01 and 2010 dates get no answer rather than an audited-normal verdict. **Closing condition:** a capture of the operator's `Calendário do mercado 2010` announcement or of the 2010 holiday Ofício Circular, at which point the window extends back to 2010-01-01.

Holidays that fell on weekends print no session change and ship no row — in the backfilled years: 01 January (2011, 2012, 2017, 2022, 2023), 25 January (2014), 21 April (2013), 07 September (2013, 2014, 2019, 2020, 2024), 12 October (2013, 2014, 2019, 2024), 02 November (2014, 2019, 2024), 15 November (2014, 2019, 2024), 20 November (2021), 24 December (2016, 2022), 25 December (2011, 2016, 2021, 2022), 31 December (2016, 2022). The operator's own statement that São Paulo holidays no longer close the market (`Desde o calendário de 2022, a bolsa do Brasil não para durante os feriados municipais e estaduais de São Paulo`) is the operator's 2025 article reading of its own 2022-2024 calendars, which state it per day: the 2022-01-25, 2023-11-20 and 2024-07-09 rows print `Haverá negociação nos mercados de renda variável …`, so those dates are audited normal and ship no row.

## Gaps and residual risks

- **executable, pre-opening window on the 11:00-open grids.** The 2010–2012 long grids carry no pre-opening phase. The 2010–2012 circulars pin the trading grid itself and the 2025 circular proves the pre-opening concept in force at the 2026-08-24 review, but nothing in the audited primary set states the pre-opening window for those grids; a 10:45–11:00 phase would be an inference from the fifteen-minute convention of the 2026-08-24 review, so under LAW-PRIMARY-SOURCES the window is omitted and reads closed. Closing condition: a B3 circular or annex for the 2010–2012 era that states the pre-opening window in session language.
- **Recurring regime.** From 2015-12-21 the profile is not a revision row but a recurring selector: circular 127/2015-DP ties the short and long grids to the Brazil/New York daylight-time relationship, so `profile_at` picks the short grid when São Paulo sits one hour behind its usual offset from New York. The 2015-12-21 constant lives in `b3.rs` as `REFERENCE_GRID` and is not a `revisions!` tuple.
- **Interpretive step, order-entry classification.** The fifteen minutes before the open collect and price a book that does not match and the first print is the opening call, so that leg is `order_entry`. The immediately preceding "Order Cancellation" window, 09:30–09:45, stays excluded from the model entirely. The remaining phases all print: closing calls cross at the auction price and the after-market windows execute.
- **Interpretive step, after-market envelopes.** The after-market envelopes merge the venue's after-market pre-opening with after-market trading rather than splitting them on an unsourced internal boundary, so they stay tradeable whole.
- **Service tier.** The consumer serves this venue live (it is one of the market-clock overview's venues), so the identity is **served** and the holiday-bearing calendar is reviewed monthly per LAW-WATCH; the 2027 closing condition above is the one tracked gap.
