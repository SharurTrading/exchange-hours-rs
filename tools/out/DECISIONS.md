## 3. Trade-date conversion

The crate's trade date is the venue-local date of a session's final close.

- **Wrapped families** (`globex_equity_index`, `globex_energy`,
  `globex_fx`, `globex_grains`, `globex_interest_rates`,
  `globex_cryptocurrency`, `globex_nikkei_225_dollar`): a halt or close
  printed on date D ends the session that opened on D-1, so the row lands
  on D. CME's own `TRADE DATE:` labels for these days name the following
  business day instead; the crate keys on the local date of the final
  close, so the crate's date is D and CME's label is recorded, not
  adopted. Every wrapped-family row in this wave has trade date = the
  block's own date, and no row needed a different conversion.
- **Flat family** (`globex_livestock`): the session lies inside one civil
  day, so the operator's event date is the crate's trade date.
- **A printed open that moves a boundary** is placed by the macro's own
  `LateOpen` rule: a value at or after the ordinary first open belongs to
  the previous local date, a value below it to the trade date itself.
  Every deviating open here is a day-session `08:30 CT` open on a wrapped
  family, so it lands on the local date the block prints for it.

## 9. Questions to rule on

**Q1. The `dairy` -> `globex_grains` fold joins two different clocks.**

`dairy`'s ordinary week is one wrapped leg per trade date, 17:00 CT the previous evening into a 16:00 CT close (13:55 CT on Fridays); `globex_grains`'s is 19:00 CT the previous evening into 07:45 CT, then the 08:30-13:20 CT day session whose close is the trading day's final closing phase, then the 14:30-16:00 CT post-close period.
